About

Making Advanced Technology Accessible to Growing Businesses.

Elitron Labs is a Michigan-based technology studio focused on building practical, intelligent digital solutions. We combine software engineering, modern design and AI automation to help businesses improve operations, serve customers and build new capabilities.

Our approach

Strategy and execution, in the same room.

Many businesses know technology could help. Fewer know where to start, and fewer still have someone who can both design the solution and build it.

That's the gap we work in. We look at how your business actually runs: where customers come from, where work gets stuck, which tools you already pay for. Then we build the smallest thing that makes a real difference. Sometimes that's a better website. Sometimes it's an automation that saves hours every week. Sometimes it's custom software.

We use AI where it genuinely helps, keep people in control where judgment matters, and explain our work in plain language.

Principles

How we work.

Engineering first

Our background spans electrical and computer engineering, software integration and automation. We design systems that hold up after launch day.

Simple beats clever

We recommend the least complex solution that solves the problem, and we'll tell you when you don't need custom work.

Honest by default

Clear scopes, clear labels on demos and concepts, and no promises about results we can't control.

Thoughtful AI

AI handles the repetitive parts. Approval steps and human escalation stay in place where they matter.

Built to improve

We ship, measure what we can, and refine, with support options that fit how much help you want.

Michigan-based

We take on projects for businesses in Michigan and beyond, remotely or in person where it's practical.
