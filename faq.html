FAQ

Straight answers before you start.

What most people ask before working with us. If your question isn't here, ask us directly.

How much does a project typically cost?

Standard websites start from $799 one-time setup, with an optional $79/month care plan. You can request a free website preview before you commit.

AI automation, AI agents and custom software are scoped and quoted after a free discovery call. The cost depends on what needs to be connected and built, so we don't publish fixed prices for them. The budget ranges on our contact form only help us suggest a sensible starting point.

How long does development take?

Standard websites can often launch within 1–2 weeks of design approval, depending on how quickly content and feedback come back.

Automation and custom software are scheduled during scoping. Your proposal lists milestones, and you review a working version at each stage.

Can you work with my existing software?

Usually, yes. We connect tools through their official APIs, webhooks or automation platforms such as n8n: CRMs, calendars, accounting, e-commerce, email and more.

During discovery we check what each of your tools actually allows. If something can't be connected reliably, we tell you and suggest an alternative rather than building something fragile.

Do I own the website or source code?

Yes. Once your project is paid in full, you own your website, your content and the custom code we write for you.

Third-party tools, hosting platforms, plugins and AI services remain with their providers under their own terms. We list them in your handover documentation.

Can AI automation be added to my existing business?

Yes. You don't need to replace your systems. Most clients start with one high-value workflow, such as following up new leads, booking appointments or processing a common document, connected to the tools they already use.

Once that is working and you trust it, we expand step by step. You can try a simplified example in our interactive automation demo.

What happens after deployment?

You get a handover walkthrough and documentation of what was built, where it runs, and how to make common changes.

Ongoing support is optional and defined in your agreement. For websites, that's the care plan. For automation and software, we agree on monitoring, fixes and improvements that fit how much help you want.

What security and maintenance responsibilities are included?

Responsibilities are written into your agreement before work starts, so it is always clear who maintains what: hosting, updates, accounts, access and data.

We build with secure defaults: least-privilege access, secrets kept out of code, validated inputs, automated tests for important logic, and human approval before sensitive actions. See how we approach security.

We do not currently hold formal security certifications such as SOC 2 or ISO 27001, and we don't claim compliance on your behalf. If your project needs them, we'll say so up front.

Still have a question?

Ask us directly. You'll get a straight answer from the person who'd build your project.
