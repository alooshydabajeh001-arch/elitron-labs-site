03 Automation in action

See What Intelligent Automation Can Do.

Follow one service request from a website form to a booked appointment. Edit the inquiry, then step through what an automated workflow does with it.

Demonstration · synthetic data
  1. 1Customer inquiry
  2. 2AI qualification
  3. 3CRM update
  4. 4Appointment booking

How the workflow runs

1. A visitor requests a service estimate. 2. The request is checked against your qualification rules. 3. A CRM record is created. 4. The customer is offered open appointment times. 5. The booking is confirmed and your team is notified.

Runs entirely in your browser with made-up data. Nothing is sent, stored or delivered to anyone. A production build connects to your real CRM and calendar, with your rules and human review where you want it.

05 Selected work

Our Work, Prototypes & Technology.

Every project is labelled for what it is: a live project, an internal build, a working prototype or a concept design.

Research prototype

VERIFAI: checking the facts inside an AI agent's actions

Before an AI agent sends a purchase order, VERIFAI checks each detail (price, quantity, part, supplier) against the source quote. If the evidence is missing, it hands the order to a person instead of guessing.

  • Let none of 13 seeded error types through; permission-style rules alone caught about 18%
  • Sends about half of clean orders on an unfamiliar quote layout to a person, the main open problem

Synthetic data only. No users or customers yet.
