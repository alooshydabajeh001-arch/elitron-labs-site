Our Work · Working prototype

Summit HVAC website concept.

A complete, working website for a fictional heating and cooling company, built to show local service businesses what a conversion-focused site looks like.

StatusWorking prototype · fictional brand
TypeWeb design · Front-end
IndustryHome services (illustrative)
Year2026

The problem

Local service businesses win or lose jobs in the first seconds on their website: can a visitor see what you do, trust you, and reach you immediately, especially on a phone?

The proposed solution

A focused landing page that puts the phone number and an estimate request one tap away, explains services plainly, and answers the questions customers ask before they call.

Features & technology

  • Click-to-call in the header, hero, closing section and a sticky mobile tap-to-call bar
  • "Book Free Estimate" calls to action throughout
  • Six-service overview, trust section and service-area section
  • Sample review section, clearly marked as sample content
  • Single self-contained HTML page: no external scripts, fast to load

The demo does not include a working booking system. In a client project, the estimate buttons would connect to a real request form, scheduling tool or the automation shown in our lead automation demo.
