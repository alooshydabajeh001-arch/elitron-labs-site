Privacy Policy

Last updated October 12, 2026

This policy explains how Elitron Labs LLC ("Elitron Labs", "we") handles information on elitronlabs.io. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • Information you send us. When you submit a form, we receive what you enter: typically your name, business or organization, email, optional phone number, website address and details about your project.
  • Technical logs. Our hosting provider records standard request data (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) to operate and secure the site.

We do not use advertising cookies, and this site does not currently run third-party analytics or tracking scripts.

How we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry, prepare a website preview or proposal you asked for, and follow up on that conversation.
  • To keep records of business communications.
  • To protect the site against spam and abuse.

We do not sell your information, and we do not add you to marketing lists from a form submission without asking.

Who processes it

We use service providers to run the site and deliver form submissions to us, such as our website host and our email or workflow tools. They process the information only to provide those services to us. We may disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry details for as long as needed to respond and maintain reasonable business records, then delete or anonymize them. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

To access, correct or delete information you sent us, email admin@elitronlabs.io. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Please don't send sensitive data

Do not submit passwords, payment card numbers, government identifiers, health information or confidential or export-controlled technical data through this website.

Children

This site is intended for businesses and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We will post updates here and change the date above.
